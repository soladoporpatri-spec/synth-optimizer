Privacy Policy and Cookies

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This policy explains how Synth Optimizer ("we") handles the personal data of people who visit synthoptimizer.com or hire our optimization services. It follows Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018, the LGPD) and, for visitors from other countries, applicable data protection laws such as the EU GDPR and the UK GDPR.

1. Who is responsible

Synth Optimizer is the controller of the data covered by this policy. To talk to us about privacy, use the channels below:

  • WhatsApp: +55 62 99448-8816
  • Instagram: @synth_optimizer

2. What data we collect

  • Contact data: your name or nickname and phone/WhatsApp number when you message us to ask for a quote or hire a plan. We may also receive your Discord, Instagram or TikTok username if you contact us there.
  • Service data: information about your computer or console (parts, operating system, the games you play) and what you expect from the service.
  • Remote access: during the session, you give us temporary access to your computer through a remote access program (such as AnyDesk). It is used only to make the agreed adjustments, with you watching the screen. We do not copy your personal files.
  • Payment: payment is processed by the method you choose. We do not store full card details.
  • Browsing data: technical server logs (such as IP address, date and time, browser, language and pages visited), needed for the site to work and stay secure. If you accept measurement cookies, we also collect usage statistics (see section 7).

3. What we use the data for

  • To answer your message, schedule a time and deliver the service you hired.
  • To bill you and meet legal and tax obligations.
  • To provide support and redo adjustments when needed.
  • To keep the site secure and working well.
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it, only with your consent (measurement cookies).

4. Legal bases

We process your data based on performance of a contract or pre-contractual steps at your request, compliance with a legal obligation, legitimate interest (site security and operation) and, for measurement cookies, your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. It may be processed by providers that make the service possible, each under their own policies:

  • Hostinger, which hosts the site.
  • WhatsApp (Meta), Discord, Instagram and TikTok, our support and outreach channels.
  • AnyDesk or a similar program, for remote access.
  • Google Fonts and cdnjs (Cloudflare), which deliver the site's fonts and libraries. When the page loads, these services may receive your IP address.
  • Google Analytics, only if you accept measurement cookies.

Some of these providers are located in other countries. As a result, your data may be transferred to and processed outside your country of residence, including in Brazil. In those cases we apply the safeguards provided by the LGPD and, where applicable, by the law of your country.

6. How long we keep data

We keep data for as long as needed to deliver the service, provide support and meet legal obligations. After that, it is deleted or anonymized. Support conversations can be deleted at your request, subject to legal retention obligations.

7. Cookies

Cookies are small files your browser stores to remember information. Our site works like this:

  • Essential: the site does not need cookies to work and does not set advertising cookies.
  • Measurement (optional): if we turn on Google Analytics, it only loads after you accept the cookie notice. You can decline without losing any site feature.
  • Your choice: we only store your answer to the notice (accept or decline) in your browser's local storage, so we do not ask again.

You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings.

8. Your rights

Under the LGPD you can ask, at any time, for: confirmation that we process your data; access; correction of incomplete or outdated data; anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data; portability; information about who we share data with; and withdrawal of consent. To do so, contact us through the channels in section 1. We will reply as soon as possible, within legal deadlines.

9. Children and teenagers

Our audience includes young gamers. People under 18 need the knowledge and authorization of a parent or guardian to hire the service and allow remote access to the computer. We always process the data of children and teenagers in their best interest.

10. Visitors from other countries

We serve customers in Brazil and in other countries, always remotely. Synth Optimizer is based in Brazil, and data is processed from Brazil and by providers in other countries (see section 5).

If you are in the European Union, the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, in addition to the rights in section 8 you may object to processing based on legitimate interest, ask us to restrict processing and lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country. In other countries, we respect the rights provided by the applicable local law. To exercise them, use the channels in section 1.

11. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect data: the site is served over HTTPS, remote access happens only with your authorization and while you watch, and access to support information is restricted. No system is completely immune to failure, but we act quickly if an incident occurs and notify those affected when the law requires.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The update date is shown at the top of this page, and relevant changes will be highlighted on the site.
